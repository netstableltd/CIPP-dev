# Review

**Reports > Review** lists customer report drafts. A draft is the customer report exactly as it would be sent; you can change it, then approve it, then send it. Create one from **Reports > Companies > Create draft for review** (choose the month). Nothing is emailed when a draft is created.

Open a draft with **Review and edit**. The PDF is on the right; on the left you can:

* write a **note from your IT team**, shown in a box on the summary page;
* untick **sections** to leave them out of this month's report only;
* untick a **recommendation** to leave it out, change its wording, or change the list of what it affects;
* **add a recommendation** of your own, as "To do now" or "To plan for".

Pre-check items that are only for your team are listed at the bottom for reference; they never appear in the customer report.

**Save and update PDF** regenerates the draft with your changes (your changes are kept if you regenerate later). **Refresh data** rebuilds it from the latest data, keeping your changes.

| Status | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Edit, save, approve or discard. |
| Approved | Send to the customer, or reopen to make more changes. Regenerating a draft always clears its approval, so the PDF you approved is the PDF that is sent. |
| Sent | Read only. Shows who it went to and when. |

**Send to customer** emails the approved PDF to the company's report recipients (Reports > Companies). It only works while **Allow sending reports to customer recipients** is on in [Settings](settings.md).

Review needs `CIPP.Reports` Read to view and Read/Write to change, approve or send, and only shows companies you have access to.
